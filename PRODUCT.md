# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step. Confirmed by the author.

## Users

The redesign is an unofficial portfolio piece by its author; the school did not commission it. Its audience is whoever reviews the portfolio.

Inside the fiction of the site the audiences are those of the real school: parents choosing a school for a child (förskoleklass or årskurs 7 especially), current parents handling everyday errands (sjukanmälan, kalendarium, lunch), and staff, former pupils and neighbours coming for events.

## Product Purpose

An improved version of https://rudolfsteinerskolan.se/ that covers the whole site. Keep the school's real content and make it a pleasure to explore. Success: a reviewer sees real craft, and a parent could still find any fact or errand faster than on the original.

## Positioning

Rudolf Steinerskolan in Norrköping is a Waldorf school founded in 1975, F-9, in a garden "oas" on Ståthöga within walking distance of the city centre. Non-profit foundation. The class teacher follows pupils for the first six years, each day starts with a morning period, and subjects are taught through art, craft and music.

## Operating Context

The school year runs on a fixed rhythm: Mikaeli, skördefest, lyktfest, julbasar, adventsträdgård, Valborg and vårfest. Parents leave for external systems: SchoolSoft, the Knack application portal, Norrköpings kommun. Locals call the site "Kullen".

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All copy stays in Swedish, taken from the real site. No invented facts, quotes, numbers or people.
- Forms cannot submit anywhere: external links go to the real services, and local forms must say plainly that they are a demonstration.
- The site must state that it is an unofficial portfolio concept and must not be indexed by search engines.
- Author's explicit requests: animated flying gulls at the top, photos that react to scrolling, and overall restraint ("не делай слишком максимализма").

## Brand Commitments

Name "Rudolf Steinerskolan", the existing bird logo and bird favicon. Tone is warm, calm and plain.

## Evidence on Hand

Real text from 26 pages, the 2026/27 kalendarium, staff list, contacts, teacher quotes and photos from the school's WordPress uploads: chalkboard drawings, watercolours, the advent spiral, garden, class trips, a music lesson. Staff portraits exist on the original site.

Photo and name rights belong to the school and the people pictured. They have not been cleared for portfolio publication. No testimonials, statistics, awards or results beyond what the site itself states.

## Product Principles

1. Errands first: sick leave, calendar and lunch are never more than one step away.
2. The school's own handmade work carries the identity, not decoration added on top.
3. Motion serves as calm, like the year's rhythm, and never competes with reading.
4. Every fact stays traceable to the original site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Must honour reduced-motion preferences. Parents use it on phones, often in a hurry.
